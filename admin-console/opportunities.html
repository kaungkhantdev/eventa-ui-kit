    How impact is estimated

    An estimate, not a forecast

    Every figure on this page is a modelled upper bound on what could be recovered — it is what the loss is worth if the friction disappeared entirely, which it never does.

    The three inputs

    1. 1Reach. Distinct workspaces that hit the step, over the last 90 days.
    2. 2Drop. The share of them that never get past it, compared with the workspaces that do.
    3. 3Value. Subscription plus take-rate difference between the two groups, annualised.

    What confidence means

    HighBehaviour reproduced in a holdout and the value gap survives a plan-size control.
    MediumPattern is clear, but the drop and the value could share a common cause.
    LowSmall sample, or the signal only shows up in one plan tier.

    Impact figures overlap. Fixing two opportunities that affect the same workspaces does not recover the sum of both — treat the total as a ceiling for the whole list, not a budget to divide up.
