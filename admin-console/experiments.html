How we read a result

The interval decides, not the number

Every result on this page is a point estimate with a 95 % confidence interval. If the interval contains zero, the test did not find an effect — however good the headline number looks.

Reading the bar

Clear of zero, good side. The change helped. Ship it.
Crossing zero. No effect was found. That is a result, not a failure to finish.
Clear of zero, bad side. The change hurt. Roll it back and write down why.

The rules we hold to

  • The metric and the sample size are fixed before the split is turned on.
  • A running test shows its interim number, and it is labelled as too early.
  • Nothing is called a win after the fact by swapping to a metric that moved.
  • Guardrails can stop a test on their own, even when the primary metric is up.

Guardrails are the metrics a test is not allowed to damage — events created, checkout completion, support contacts. They are checked daily and breach ends the test.
